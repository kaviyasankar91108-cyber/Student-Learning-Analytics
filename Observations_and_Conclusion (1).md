## Interpretation
Students who study for more hours, complete more assignments, and maintain higher attendance generally achieve higher examination marks in this dataset. The scatter plot is the most suitable visualization for examining the relationship between study hours and marks because it displays each student's paired values.

Students needing additional academic support are S205 and S212, who scored below 50. S207 scored exactly 50 and is classified as Good under the given criteria, but may also benefit from monitoring.

## Suggestions
1. Provide additional coaching and doubt-clearing sessions for students scoring below 50.
2. Encourage a regular daily study schedule and revision plan.
3. Track assignment completion and provide timely reminders.
4. Encourage consistent class attendance.
5. Review progress through periodic quizzes and feedback.

## Conclusion
The sample indicates that better study habits, assignment completion, and attendance are associated with higher examination performance. These are associations in this 12-student dataset and do not by themselves prove that any one factor causes higher marks. Regular monitoring and targeted academic support may help improve learning outcomes.
